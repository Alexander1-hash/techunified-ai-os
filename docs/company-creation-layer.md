# TechUnified Company Creation Layer

The Company Creation Layer is the guided entry point for turning a business idea into a structured company setup plan inside TechUnified AI OS.

It is an orchestration layer, not a claim that TechUnified itself is a government registrar, bank, trademark office, legal adviser, or other regulated provider.

## Workflow

1. Company idea
2. Company Creation Agreement
3. Company identity
4. Formation and compliance plan
5. Digital infrastructure
6. Launch plan
7. Connection to Company Brain and the service marketplace

## Commercial model

Potential revenue streams include subscriptions, service fees, provider commissions, marketplace transaction fees, and contractual equity arrangements.

Any equity arrangement must be implemented through a jurisdiction-appropriate legal agreement. The UI therefore treats the 5% concept as a proposed commercial term rather than silently creating a binding equity right.

## Principles

- Explain every step in plain language.
- Show what is happening and why.
- Never imply a third-party service is complete until verified.
- Keep providers modular.
- Keep customer company ownership distinct from TechUnified contractual rights.
- Record agreement versions, acceptance timestamps, jurisdiction, and exact presented terms.
- Connect verified company data to the existing Company Brain.
