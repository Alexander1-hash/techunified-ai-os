# Company Creation Layer Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Guided UI
- Agreement presentation
- Project persistence
- Company identity
- Task engine
- Audit timeline

## Phase 2 — Intelligence

- Company Architect agent
- Intent extraction
- Dynamic questions
- Jurisdiction-aware task generation
- Next-best-action explanations

## Phase 3 — Provider marketplace

- Domain providers
- Company formation providers
- Trademark providers
- Accounting providers
- Website and brand services
- Payment providers

## Phase 4 — Payments

- Checkout
- Provider fee breakdown
- TechUnified service fee or commission
- Receipts
- Refund status
- Payment reconciliation

## Phase 5 — Company Brain

- Feed verified company data into Company Brain
- Generate operational recommendations
- Create workflows and automations from completed setup
- Connect AI agents to company context

## Phase 6 — Scale

- Multi-jurisdiction rules
- Provider routing
- Partner APIs
- Enterprise onboarding
- Contract and equity lifecycle management
